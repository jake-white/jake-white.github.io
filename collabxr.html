CollabXR

My Role: Lead Developer, Designer

CollabXR Website

CollabXR is a multi-user, colocated XR experience designed for use in education and research. I have been the primary developer on this project for several years and have successfully facilitated its usage in numerous classes across Purdue.

Across the development of Collab, I have worked on many novel features such as hassle-free colocation, loading user-generated content via Assetbundles, and 3D point cloud rendering techniques.

Content I worked spans many disclipines, including astrophysics, meteorology, anatomy, engineering, aviation, nuclear pharmacy, and more.
