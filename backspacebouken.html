Backspace Bouken

My Role: Lead Programmer, Designer, Writer

Published December 2019

Backspace Bouken was my first shipped commercial game, created by myself and 2 friends. I was responsible for all of the game's programming. The game uses a unique 2.5D rendering style that required some convoluted choreography within the game engine. I also wrote almost the entire script, which explains why it's so ridiculous.
